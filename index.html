Vision 2029

Where We Are Headed

By 2029 we want to be a healthy, growing, and multiplying church with at least 700 people in Cottesloe and an active church planting movement seeing disciples made and churches planted in Perth, WA, and beyond.

St Philips describes this mission through discipleship that reaches every generation, deep biblical prayer, servant leadership, and everyday witness that extends Jesus' kingdom in homes, schools, workplaces, and neighbourhoods.

Gospel Word Deep confidence in the Bible as God's word and the heart of church life.
Gospel Community Intentional, loving fellowship where people are known and cared for.
Gospel Culture Prayerful holiness, generosity, service, and faithful witness in daily life.
Gospel Multiplication Making disciples who make disciples and planting churches that plant churches.

Church Planting & Mission

St Philips has a long-term commitment to local and global church planting through partnerships, leadership development, and practical support.

Local Church Plant

The Common, East Vic Park

Planted in 2014 under Craig and Karen Hamilton and now led by James and Annabeth Parnell.

Kenya Partnership

Kalobeyei, Pochalla & Okadi

Support for church planting and leadership training, including St Philip's Academy with more than 250 children and eight teachers.

Training

The Incubator

Hosted with St Luke's Shenton Park to train apprentice ministers, church planters, and lay leaders.

Revitalisation

St Matthew's Guildford

St Philips has been helping St Matthew's with renewal work and a long-term pathway toward healthy replanting.
